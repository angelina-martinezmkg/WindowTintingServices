Why tint

More than a darker window.

  • Helps reduce heat

    Quality film can help keep cabins and rooms more comfortable on sunny days.

  • UV protection

    Window film is designed to help block UV rays coming through the glass.

  • Less glare

    Cut down harsh sun and headlight glare for easier driving and screen viewing.

  • Added privacy

    Keep what's inside your car, home or office a little more private.

  • Interior protection

    Help reduce sun fading on seats, dashboards, floors and furniture.

  • A sleek, finished look

    Clean edges and an even shade that make any vehicle or building look sharper.
